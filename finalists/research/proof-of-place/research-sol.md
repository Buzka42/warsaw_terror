## 1. GNSS: two different experiments, neither a capture seal

| Severity | Finding and evidence | What to do before submission |
|---|---|---|
| **BLOCKER** | **OSNMA authenticates Galileo navigation data, not the range measurements, receiver position, camera, or shutter time.** It uses delayed key disclosure and requires a receiver with a trustworthy time relationship to Galileo System Time. Four authenticated satellites and agreement with a phone fix are not proof: both fixes may share untrustworthy measurements, and the quality of the geometry still matters. Copernicus does not close this gap: an incorrect claim can point into a genuine flood. C/N₀, AGC and multi-constellation checks are diagnostics, not a security guarantee. Galileo’s SDD distinguishes future ranging-authentication capabilities from OSNMA; do not sell them as a weekend dependency or a complete replay defence. ([gsc-europa.eu](https://www.gsc-europa.eu/sites/default/files/Galileo-OSNMA-SDD_v1.0.pdf?utm_source=openai)) | Delete `AUTHENTICATED POSITION`, `gst_utc` as a shutter-time claim, and the four-satellite pass rule. Show `NAV DATA AUTHENTICATED`, `INSUFFICIENT DATA`, or `NOT EVALUATED`, with input type, recording epoch, satellite IDs and time-source assumptions. If a Galileo-only position happens to work, label it **“position computed using authenticated navigation data; ranging not authenticated.”** A moved pin is a **claim edit**, not a failed OSNMA check. |
| **MAJOR** | **Android API support is not proof of usable data from your phone.** Google’s GnssLogger exports text/CSV and can export RINEX measurements; Android defines `GnssNavigationMessage.TYPE_GAL_I`. OSNMAlib’s Android reader is for **postprocessing**. Its documentation describes timestamps inferred from `Raw`/`Agc` records, chipset-specific handling, and ambiguity between E1-B and E5b-I in `Nav` records. A `Fix` line or RINEX observation file alone does not supply the required raw I/NAV pages. ([developer.android.com](https://developer.android.com/develop/sensors-and-location/sensors/gnss?utm_source=openai)) | Give one person one outdoor session Saturday morning. Record handset model, chipset if known, app version and Android build; inspect actual Galileo `Nav` plus time-bearing rows, then demand successful authentication in the library’s JSON output. **Stop at noon.** Fall back to `Algafix/OSNMA` → `custom_run/current_config.sbf` → `python run.py`, pin the tested commit and save the output. The repo supports Python 3.11 and is EUPL-1.2. A borrowed Septentrio with `GALRawINAV` SBF is useful only if it is already in hand; documented u-blox UBX support is not a guarantee for an arbitrary model or firmware. No receiver purchases or SDR work this weekend. ([github.com](https://github.com/Algafix/OSNMA?utm_source=openai)) |
| **MAJOR** | **Trust material and acquisition time are real dependencies.** GSC provides current and historical public keys, certificates and Merkle material after login. The library includes a matched sample configuration and documents the December 2025 key transition. OSNMA Initial Service began **24 July 2025**; September 2024 was the public observation test phase. The SDD’s ADKD0 95% targets are 130 s hot and 300 s warm under stated conditions; its cold-start target when starting from the Merkle root is far longer. The library’s reported 44-second optimized hot start is not a phone SLA. ([gsc-europa.eu](https://www.gsc-europa.eu/galileo/faq?utm_source=openai)) | Start with the matched offline fixture. Obtain official material for the epoch you actually process, record the key ID and service status, and show replay as replay. Do not attach a 2026 log to a 2024 file or imply the historical asset was authenticated. No stage cold start, no live Galmon dependency. |
| **BLOCKER** | **EGNOS does not bound the proposed Galileo-only solution.** In-force EGNOS Open Service v3.0 augments **GPS L1 C/A**, not Galileo. The published 3 m horizontal figure is a 95% service-performance figure under specified conditions, not an individual-phone bound. RTKLIB covariance, Android’s 68% estimated radius and a Safety-of-Life horizontal protection level are three different things. Even a working SBAS GPS solution does not confer integrity on a separate Galileo position. ([egnos.gsc-europa.eu](https://egnos.gsc-europa.eu/documents/egnos-open-service-sdd?utm_source=openai)) | Delete `egnos_error_bound_m`, the `±4 m` badge and every court-grade or safety-of-life claim. Report the solution difference and epochs actually corrected; call it improvement only if evaluated against an independent reference. Keep it outside the seal and the EO search radius. |
| **MAJOR** | **RTKLIB exists; the phone-to-SBAS pipeline does not come with it.** `rnx2rtkp` can postprocess RINEX observations/navigation and compatible SBAS logs, including EMS 2.0 per the manual. Upstream lists `2.4.3 b34` as a beta baseline. It does not take GnssLogger CSV plus OSNMAlib JSON and return a time-aligned, per-epoch Galileo solution limited to authenticated ephemerides. The legacy ESA EMS listing documents free hourly FTP archives, not verified access to the needed hour. ([rtklib.com](https://www.rtklib.com/?utm_source=openai)) | Use a known working RTKLIB binary only if you already have overlapping GPS RINEX OBS/NAV and EGNOS EMS/SBS files. Run with SBAS off/on and check that corrections were consumed rather than silently falling back. No matched files by noon Saturday means **omit EGNOS**, not “coming online later.” Do not build the Galileo PVT join on the critical path. |

## 2. Copernicus: make this the working product

| Severity | Finding and evidence | What to do before submission |
|---|---|---|
| **MAJOR** | **GFM access is documented; the proposed clip match is not.** The public STAC collection `GFM` exposes Sentinel-1 IW GRD-derived products as cloud-optimized GeoTIFFs, with an archive advertised from 2015. WMS-T at `https://geoserver.gfm.eodc.eu/geoserver/gfm/wms` is suitable for display; rendered map colours are not the analytical raster. If an asset fails or no useful pass covers the point, switch to EMSR756, not to a last-minute SAR classifier. |
| **BLOCKER** | **“Satellite looked, saw nothing” is not “inconsistent.”** GFM has **20 m pixel sampling**, not 20 m certainty or a guarantee of street-level detection. Radar can acquire through clouds, but terrain shadow, layover, built-up areas, vegetation, permanent water and other exclusions or ambiguity matter. A camera on dry ground may film water across the river. The proposed `max(3 × error, resolution)` has no validated statistical meaning; a wider search and ±72 h also make chance matches easier. The sample’s 118 m positive does not meet its own stated 60 m example radius. ([wiki.c-scale.eu](https://wiki.c-scale.eu/gfm_assets/gfm_pdd_v4_0.pdf?utm_source=openai)) | Use four states: **`FLOOD MAPPED NEAR CLAIM`**, **`NO FLOOD MAPPED IN USABLE COVERAGE`**, **`NO USABLE OBSERVATION`**, and **`DATA UNAVAILABLE`**. Display the analyst’s search radius and time window, the distance to the closest mapped area, and the signed time gap—not a green truth badge. Predeclare the demo settings; calculate metres in a projected CRS, not degrees. A partially masked search area cannot support a blanket negative. |
| **MAJOR** | **“In seconds” describes a cached lookup, not satellite availability.** Sentinel-1C/1D restored nominal **six-day interferometric repeat on 24 June 2026**; Sentinel-1A ended operations **29 June 2026**. GFM advertises availability within about eight hours **after acquisition**, not after a flood begins or a photo is taken. ([sentinels.copernicus.eu](https://sentinels.copernicus.eu/-/sentinel-1c/1d-final-orbital-configuration-achieved?utm_source=openai)) | Show three times: claimed capture, satellite acquisition, and product publication/availability if known. For an as-of replay, use only data demonstrably available by the selected cutoff or explicitly label it retrospective. Benchmark the local lookup separately from acquisition and publication lag. |
| **MAJOR** | **EMSR756 is a good fallback, not ground truth.** CEMS activated it on **13 September 2024**. The bulletin lists Kłodzko **AOI13**, Stronie Śląskie **AOI19**, and Głuchołazy **AOI20**, each with one product by the bulletin date, and Lądek-Zdrój **AOI18** still in progress in that bulletin. Those listed dates are not automatically sensing times. Early Świdnica/Jelenia Góra delineations used COSMO-SkyMed imagery; a CEMS product is a legitimate Copernicus service output, but not necessarily Sentinel-1. The Polish Space Agency explicitly cautions that the maps show water at acquisition, not maximum extent. ([mapping.emergency.copernicus.eu](https://mapping.emergency.copernicus.eu/news/flood-in-south-west-poland/)) | Query `https://rapidmapping.emergency.copernicus.eu/backend/dashboard-api/public-activations/?code=EMSR756`; use the returned AOI product’s `downloadPath` and metadata, not a guessed ZIP name. If the API fails, use the activation viewer or POLSA NSIS GeoPackage export. Cache one **flood delineation plus its coverage footprint**, version, source sensor, sensing time and delivery time. Outside the footprint is unknown; outside a polygon is not verified dry land. Attribute the CEMS product and check terms before redistributing any underlying third-party imagery. ([mapping.emergency.copernicus.eu](https://mapping.emergency.copernicus.eu/about/how-to-harvest-cems-mapping-data/emergency-response-data/)) |
| **MAJOR** | **Raw Sentinel-1 thresholding is not an independent second observation and is a poor fallback.** The documented CDSE openEO collection is `SENTINEL1_GRD`; the Python client is `openeo`, with OIDC at `https://openeo.dataspace.copernicus.eu` or `https://openeofed.dataspace.copernicus.eu`. `sar_backscatter` is available for documented workflows, but a defensible before/after comparison needs comparable geometry/polarization, calibrated backscatter with consistent units, terrain handling and masks. ([documentation.dataspace.copernicus.eu](https://documentation.dataspace.copernicus.eu/APIs/OData.html?utm_source=openai)) | **Cut fire, ±12 h verdicts and the LSTM roadmap slide.** If revisited later, `xarray` plus a NetCDF backend such as `netCDF4` can read downloaded files; first verify the actual product variables and quality flags. ## 3. C2PA, implementation and the stage demo

| Severity | Finding and evidence | What to do before submission |
|---|---|---|
| **BLOCKER** | **Signing an uploaded photo and a loose log does not bind either to the capture event.** A valid C2PA signature binds an assertion to an asset under the signer’s key; it does not establish that the assertion is true. A mixed-and-matched JPEG and real log can still produce a valid backend signature. Google does document Content Credentials across Pixel 10 Pixel Camera photos; that does not imply this app inherits the same capture trust, or that credentials identify what a camera was pointed at. ([spec.c2pa.org](https://spec.c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/2.5/specs/ContentCredentials.html?utm_source=openai)) | **Sign an analyst receipt**, preferably a team-generated JPEG evidence card. Record the asset hash if an original is available, claimed point/time, source IDs, observation UTC, query settings, coverage, result and issue time as distinct fields. Mark `capture_binding: not_established`. For unsealed third-party media, do not present a new manifest as an original capture credential. A copied or stripped file should display **no credential available**, not **fake**. |
| **MAJOR** | **C2PA signing is feasible, but parsing JSON is not validation.** PyPI lists `c2pa-python==0.39.0` (12 November 2026), imported as `c2pa`, Python ≥3.10, MIT/Apache-2.0, with native wheels. The maintained interfaces use `Builder.sign_file(...)` and `Reader`; old free-function examples are stale. A test certificate is not trusted by default, and a generic viewer need not render your custom assertion. An internet timestamp service adds a stage dependency and timestamps signing, not capture. ([pypi.org](https://pypi.org/project/c2pa-python/)) | Sign **one JPEG** with an app-owned reverse-domain assertion and a development PEM signer. Keep the key off Git. In the local verifier, check active manifest, signature, hard binding and expected signer separately; show **“valid asset binding / demo signer”** only when warranted. Test an untouched receipt, changed image content, and a no-manifest export. Use `c2patool` as the integration fallback if the Python wheel fails; if neither signs and verifies, show an **unsigned** receipt rather than simulating the signature. |
| **MINOR** | **The application stack is available; most of it is unnecessary.** Python 3.11 with `fastapi`, `uvicorn` and `python-multipart` suffices for a local upload UI. `rasterio`/`numpy` handle raster crops and masks; `shapely`/`pyproj` handle geometry and metre distances. `xarray` adds no value for a single cropped GeoTIFF. Both `maplibre-gl` and `leaflet` exist; MapLibre adds WebGL and can add style, glyph and tile dependencies. ([pypi.org](https://pypi.org/project/fastapi/?utm_source=openai)) | Use one known laptop, one Python 3.11 venv, wheel installs and a frozen lockfile after a clean smoke test. Pick vendored **Leaflet 1.9.4** plus local overlays, or skip the basemap and serve a static georeferenced graphic. Keep only `/claim-check` and `/verify`, with local signing behind the receipt action. Bind to localhost; cap uploads, restrict to JPEG, and do not expose a public signing endpoint or `/intake` this weekend. |
| **BLOCKER** | **The Valencia demo asks the wrong question.** Full Fact found four of five clips posted before the Spanish floods, with captions pointing to Poland; it did not trace the fifth or independently establish survey-grade locations for the others. **Valencia also flooded.** A genuine flood observation there can make the wrong caption environmentally plausible. CEMS’s own Polish AOI records also show that the exact town and date are not guaranteed to yield a usable delineation. ([fullfact.org](https://fullfact.org/online/spain-flooding-compilation-poland-september/)) | Use Full Fact as the hook and attribute the **earlier-publication evidence to Full Fact**. Say “an earlier upload contradicts the later-event caption; our satellite check only adds context.” Then run the product on an independently sourced, rights-cleared Polish case and a known coverage-gap fixture. If you can reproduce a case where both places flooded, that is an excellent demo of why you abstain—not a result to hide. |
| **MAJOR** | **The current stage plan has too many live failure points.** GNSS needs suitable reception and time; EO requests can fail or produce no usable pass; C2PA trust or missing credentials can change the UI outcome; the five minutes after submission are not a testing window. ([gsc-europa.eu](https://www.gsc-europa.eu/sites/default/files/Galileo-OSNMA-SDD_v1.0.pdf)) | **Run the core demo with Wi-Fi off:** local app, local data, local CSS/JS, original/signed/changed fixtures, optional prerecorded GNSS file, MP4 and PDF fallback. Show one mapped-flood result and one abstention, then verify the receipt. A short Saturday outdoor recording is optional; kill the planned Sunday 13:00 stage dependency. Freeze Sunday at noon, rehearse twice, publish by **14:15 CET**. |
