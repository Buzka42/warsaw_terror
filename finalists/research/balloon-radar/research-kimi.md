## 1. What the design gets right

ECMWF Open Data is therefore a contingency, not the default. [CAMS dataset](https://ads.atmosphere.copernicus.eu/datasets/cams-global-atmospheric-composition-forecasts), [live retrieve schema](https://ads.atmosphere.copernicus.eu/api/retrieve/v1/processes/cams-global-atmospheric-composition-forecasts).

- **SondeHub and Legionowo are real, usable validation inputs.** Site `12374` has nominal 00 and 12 UTC launches. [SondeHub sites API](https://api.v2.sondehub.org/sites), [site documentation](https://github.com/projecthorus/sondehub-infra/wiki/Sites).
