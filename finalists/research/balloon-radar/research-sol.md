### Model and alert logic

- **BLOCKER — No detection, no calibrated probability, no all-clear.** The public records establish incidents, not a source of continuous classified or authorized tracks, a representative flight-profile distribution, or a measured miss rate. A single time and position do not determine vertical state or even establish that the object is a balloon. Five hundred draws from invented priors do not make `0.72` an operational probability; missing and undetected objects are outside that denominator. The published EYVI procedures also show why a runway rectangle cannot stand in for controlled airspace or actual traffic. **Fix:** use **named, unweighted exercise scenarios**, report `members crossing / members evaluated`, show invalid/unresolved members separately, and make **“insufficient data”** a first-class outcome. No detection claim, all-clear, recommended closure, reopening timer, or live operational alert. ([lrv.lt](https://lrv.lt/media/viesa/saugykla/2025/12/0cdYviFZDZg.pdf))
- **MAJOR — Keep the simple physics; fix the geometry and semantics.** Horizontal advection and a midpoint integrator are reasonable first approximations. Wind shear, uncertain height and time, unresolved near-ground flow, and object/track misidentification dominate the error budget. The stated chi-square multipliers are correct **for bivariate-normal contours**; a mean/covariance ellipse can cover empty space between two clusters and is not a calibrated prediction region. A window computed from *first entries* is not occupancy or clearance, and multiplying per-object non-hit rates assumes independence that shared winds do not justify. **Fix:** show individual paths or endpoint clusters; compare coarse and half-size time steps on an analytic constant-wind fixture. In the exercise zone, check segment crossings and altitude overlap, and show occupied time bins rather than a closure countdown. Add one stress case where the result stays unresolved. Use `shapely` in projected metres, `pyproj` with explicit axis order, and UTC throughout. ([cds.climate.copernicus.eu](https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/reanalysis-era5-pressure-levels?tab=overview&utm_source=openai))
- **MAJOR — Runway data are not procedure data.** The public OurAirports repo provides `runways.csv` and `airports.csv` without an account; it is an open community data source. Oro Navigacija’s **EYVI AD 2 eAIP effective 29 October 2026** is the source for published runway, SID/STAR and approach information. Neither tells the prototype what procedure or traffic configuration is active. **Fix:** verify the runway label against the eAIP, then draw **one visibly labelled exercise zone**, not a claimed real approach corridor. Remove the other airports. Name coordinate fields `lon` and `lat` or use GeoJSON `[lon, lat]`; don’t reuse the example’s ambiguous `center` array. ([github.com](https://github.com/davidmegginson/ourairports-data))

## 3. Validation and stage failure plan

**MAJOR — A 12 UTC sounding is not a 13:00 CET launch appointment.** IMGW confirms Legionowo as a sounding station. The cited Polish AIP lists **11:15 UTC (12:15 CET)** among its nominal launch times, with listed deviations and additional launches possible; SondeHub likewise distinguishes sounding cycle time from physical release time. Neither guarantees that a particular flight appears in the community receiver network, remains tracked to the ground, or lands before the pitch. **Fix:** choose a complete-enough archived flight on Saturday morning; Sunday live telemetry is optional. ([modele.imgw.pl](https://modele.imgw.pl/pomiary?category=AERO&param=Legionowo&utm_source=openai))

- **MAJOR — SondeHub access and measurement.** The public v2 REST base is `https://api.v2.sondehub.org`; use `GET /sites` to resolve the site ID, then `GET /sondes/site/{site}?last=86400`, or the documented `GET /sondes` position filter. The site endpoint supports at most seven days of lookback. Older per-serial data are documented at `https://history.sondehub.org/serial/{serial}.json.gz`; the archive can lag up to 24 hours. The API spec lists **CC BY-SA 2.0**; attribute the fixture and check redistribution terms before packaging data publicly. No weekend quota or SLA was verified. **Fix:** fetch once, deduplicate by serial/frame or measurement time, cache UTC/lat/lon/alt and retain measurement versus receipt time. Mark stale data. Call the final point **last received fix**, not landing, unless a recovery or other evidence establishes touchdown. No streaming dependency. ([github.com](https://github.com/projecthorus/sondehub-infra/blob/main/swagger.yaml))
- **MAJOR — A radiosonde check is not smuggling-balloon validation.** A model driven by the observed altitude profile is a **conditional horizontal-drift check**, not a blind full-flight forecast. ERA5 assimilates observations, so independence from the sounding cannot be presumed. One in/out result cannot calibrate a 90% region. **Fix:** freeze the input prefix and vertical assumptions before viewing the held-out suffix; report horizontal error at matched timestamps, number of points, and comparison to **constant velocity from the same prefix**. If using observed altitude throughout, say so. If using ERA5, say **hindcast**, not advance warning. SondeHub’s Tawhiri/GFS predictor is optional context, not truth; its hosted service is non-commercial and warns of performance problems. NOAA HYSPLIT is prior art, not a required integration. ([cds.climate.copernicus.eu](https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/reanalysis-era5-pressure-levels?tab=overview&utm_source=openai))
- **MAJOR — Too many moving parts for a 75-second demo.** Use Python **3.11**, `numpy`, `xarray`, `scipy`, and only the geospatial dependencies actually needed. `shapely` 2.x, `pyproj` 3.x and `rasterio` 1.4.x are real packages; native GEOS/PROJ/GDAL and ecCodes compatibility are the risks. Lock **the set that runs on the pitch laptop**, not whatever is newest. `fastapi` plus `uvicorn` can serve one local `POST /forecast`, but a CLI that regenerates JSON and a localhost static page are enough. Vendor Leaflet JS/CSS locally; MapLibre GL JS adds GPU/style/source dependencies. Use local GeoJSON, no CDN/remote tiles. Run the workflow in airplane mode and bring MP4 plus PDF screenshots. ([numpy.org](https://numpy.org/doc/stable/))
- **MINOR — A phone ping proves delivery to a chat service, not operational integration.** Telegram `sendMessage` needs a bot token and chat ID; Discord `POST /webhooks/{id}/{token}` needs an authorized webhook. Either can fail due to permissions, rate limits or connectivity; the URL/token is a secret. **Fix:** show a local, timestamped **exercise event**. If a chat ping survives scope cut, send synthetic data only and never put the secret in the repo or recording. ([core.telegram.org](https://core.telegram.org/bots/api))

**Stage sequence:** dated incident evidence → one offline replay with the observed radiosonde suffix hidden, then revealed → synthetic incident with two visibly different scenarios and an unresolved case → one buyer and one pilot ask. Leave the live tab closed unless it is already working.
