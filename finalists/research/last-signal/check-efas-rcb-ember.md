- **What we found** (with source URLs)
  - **Alert RCB Poland, 2026 = location-based SMS, not cell broadcast.** RCB’s own FAQ calls it “a new SMS-based system”, used only for high likelihood of direct threat to life/health over a significant area, based on ministries, police, fire, Border Guard, IMGW and regional authorities, with RCB monitoring 24/7. https://www.gov.pl/web/rcb-en/rcb-alert--faq
  - **Trigger/content/targeting:** No sign-up, operators must send to all users in the specified area, no app needed. Smallest area is a county; targeting is by BTS/cell-tower location, not GNSS pinpoint; phone must be on and in range; free, no opt-out, all subscribers in area receive it.
  - **Limitations (2026):** SMS = one message per subscriber, delivery depends on operator network capacity and can take time for millions of people; planned cell broadcast would send simultaneously geographically and can sound on silent. Poland is only *working towards* cell broadcast **by September 2027** per EENA 14/10/2026 — this is secondary reporting, I found no gov.pl date page. https://eena.org/newsletter/eena-update-14-10-2026/

  - **Galileo EWSS ≠ RCB and ≠ operational in Nov 2026.** EUSPA lists EWSS as broadcasting alerts via national civil-protection authorities to smartphones/Galileo devices. https://www.euspa.europa.eu/eu-space-programme/galileo/services The European Commission describes EWSS as *upcoming*, satellites transmitting hazard type/severity/onset/duration/target area/instructions generated by national authorities, on top of existing cell broadcast/SMS/sirens, usable when mobile networks are congested/disrupted/destroyed. https://defence-industry-space.ec.europa.eu/galileo-emergency-warning-satellite-service-underway-2024-01-24_en

  - **EFAS notifications are NOT public warnings:** They go to EFAS partners/authorities; public warning is the national authority’s job; forecasts/notifications have a 30-day embargo before public access. Do not describe EFAS → public directly.
