# Last Signal — technical and commercial review

**Warsaw, Saturday, 28 November 2026. Submission: Sunday, 29 November, 15:00 CET. Local pitches: 15:05 CET.** The official Warsaw agenda confirms both times. ([cassini.eu](https://www.cassini.eu/hackathons/poland))

**Build the alert-targeting workbench. Do not make the Android reference receiver a deliverable.** The credible demo is: **a real Copernicus flood footprint → an auditable comparison of encodable Galileo alert areas → a locally decoded test payload.** The original pitch promises three things the design does not establish: that the team can get an alert onto Galileo, that its phone can receive and authenticate that alert, and that the 2024 satellite map could have warned people before the flood. None is necessary to win Challenge 2. ([cassini.eu](https://www.cassini.eu/hackathons/poland))

**Severity:** **BLOCKER** means the stated deliverable or claim must be fixed or removed before submission. **MAJOR** means it can invalidate a result, undermine the business case, or break the demo. **MINOR** means it is worth fixing but not worth expanding scope.

## 1. Galileo, CAMF and the receiver

| Severity | Finding and evidence | What to do before Sunday |
|---|---|---|
| **BLOCKER — service status and uplink** | Galileo Service Notice **#28 / SNGU 2026006** confirms **sporadic** test EWM transmissions from **23 July through 30 November 2026**. It does not provide a Warsaw pass schedule, an on-demand test service, or public uplink access. The September 2026 ION programme paper puts Initial Service in **2027**; that is a plan, not a service declaration or an OEM rollout date. Authorised national civil-protection authorities are the intended originators. ([gsc-europa.eu](https://www.gsc-europa.eu/sites/default/files/sites/all/files/Galileo-service-notice-28-v1.0.pdf)) | Rename **Send** to **Export exercise draft**. Remove “Galileo can now reach that phone” and “the warning will arrive.” Say “Galileo is testing a complementary warning channel for compatible receivers.” No network send button, no real recipient list, no live transmission dependency. |
| **BLOCKER — authentication** | The ranking is right about **ordinary** OSNMA and wrong if read as saying EWSS cannot be authenticated: Notice #28 explicitly assigns **ADKD=2 to EWM authentication** in the test. The published OSNMA ICD treats it as reserved; the notice tells ordinary receivers to discard reserved tags after the relevant MAC lookup-table check. The inspected `Algafix/OSNMA` implementation’s default `ACTIVE_ADKD` is `{0,4,12}`, and its verification path skips inactive ADKDs. An OSNMA dashboard listing ADKD 2, observing a tag, or authenticating navigation on the same satellite is **not** verification of the alert. Nor does navigation authentication prove receiver position, prevent jamming, or establish that the hazard is real. ([gsc-europa.eu](https://www.gsc-europa.eu/sites/default/files/sites/all/files/Galileo-service-notice-28-v1.0.pdf)) | Delete the green integrity badge and the Challenge 1 claim. Use distinct provenance states: **local exercise fixture**, **recorded navigation only**, and, if actually obtained, **recorded test EWM — authentication not verified**. Do not patch a config switch and call it verification. Keep ordinary OSNMA out of the MVP. |
| **BLOCKER — Android reception** | Google’s `GnssLogger` and `android.location.GnssNavigationMessage` exist. The latter is API 24+; the documented Galileo I/NAV representation is **228 bits in 29 bytes**, MSB first, with four trailing padding bits. That is an API representation, not proof a handset exports usable E1-B return-link data. The callback requires fine location, enabled GNSS and a foreground client; the OSNMAlib authors also document the E1-B/E5b ambiguity in the logger format they tested. A normal GNSS fix or a `Raw` record does not prove navigation-page access, and foreground logging does not prove background or lock-screen warning support. ([developer.android.com](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/location/GnssNavigationMessage)) | One **20-minute outdoor capture** in parallel on available phones: record handset/build/logger version, constellation, `Nav` record count, SVIDs, page lengths and continuity. Save the unmodified log. If no usable result by the Saturday gate, drop radio from the demo. If the team only has ordinary pages, show them separately; never splice generated bits into the footage and call the result received. |
| **MAJOR — signal framing and receiver alternatives** | CNES describes EWSS on **Galileo E1-B I/NAV** using a **160-bit long return-link message**. The OS SIS ICD **v2.2, November 2025**, describes the **22-bit SAR data field per nominal E1-B page** and long-RLM framing. Neither a 122-bit CAMF body nor a single `Nav` callback is a complete EWSS receiver interface. ETSI **TS 104 297** was still listed in drafting on **27 November 2026**. I found no public, independently usable EWSS test capture and matching end-to-end receiver vectors to put on the critical path. ([cnes.fr](https://cnes.fr/sites/default/files/2025-12/RTN-2025_Poster_61_Emergency_Warning_Satellite_Service.pdf)) | Ask ChipCraft for a **shareable capture plus its expected decoded fields**, not a receiver procurement recommendation. `Algafix/OSNMA` supports postprocessed GnssLogger, Septentrio SBF, u-blox UBX and some GNSS-SDR inputs; none proves that a specific setup preserves or assembles EWSS. Its EUPL-1.2 code and GSC/EUSPA trust material are available for ordinary OSNMA research, but trust material matching the recording and a credible time reference are still required. No new SDR or receiver integration this weekend. ([github.com](https://github.com/Algafix/OSNMA?utm_source=openai)) |
| **BLOCKER — “official v1.2 encoding” is not yet established** | GSC lists **CAMF v1.2, March 2026**, as in force. I verified the listing, but could not reliably retrieve the body of that PDF to verify the v1.1→v1.2 diff, a complete worked vector, or the current language tables. The superseded v1.1 supports the design’s broad shape: **122-bit payload, 49-bit primary ellipse**, plus a **17-bit settings field** that can select refinement, hazard centre, secondary ellipse, or hazard detail. It contains **field-level examples**; that is not evidence of a full conformance suite. ([gsc-europa.eu](https://www.gsc-europa.eu/electronic-library/programme-reference-documents/galileo-in-force/ewss)) | Get the PDF from GSC or the mentor and record its hash. Compare §§2–3 and Annex C against the implementation. Ship **one flood test profile**, one primary ellipse and one verified settings choice; omit the other combinations. Hand-check an independent expected field breakdown and serialized bytes, including the bit length and padding convention. Add malformed/reserved-field, week-boundary, expiry and inside/outside tests. If you cannot verify v1.2 by midday, show **“CAMF geometry and experimental codec — not v1.2 validated.”** An encoder and decoder agreeing with each other is not conformance. |
| **MAJOR — display semantics** | The CAMF concept filters at the **receiver** using country/region **and** ellipse; it does not beam a signal only to the ellipse or prove a person was notified. In v1.1, the secondary ellipse has its own constrained geometry and guidance semantics; it is not a second freely fitted equivalent evacuation zone. Guidance is coded, not arbitrary text. The v1.1 list includes Polish and English but does not list Ukrainian. The week-relative onset and coded duration also make a 2024 replay using the current phone clock misleading. ([gsc-europa.eu](https://www.gsc-europa.eu/sites/default/files/sites/all/files/EWSS-CAMF_v1.1.pdf)) | Use fixed replay UTC and mock in-valley coordinates, visibly labelled. Load a local Poland boundary from GUGiK PRG; test inside, outside, wrong country, expired, and unknown library version. Use **one verified guidance entry**, not a hard-coded translation of the pitch line. Show Polish/English only if the tables are verified; Ukrainian can be a clearly labelled UI translation, not proof of official library support. Do not claim full update/all-clear behaviour. ([geoportal.gov.pl](https://www.geoportal.gov.pl/en/data/national-register-of-boundaries/?utm_source=openai)) |

**What the design gets right:** the small message budget forces a real targeting trade-off; a payload-level decoder can independently show what the proposed geometry means; and the satellite path is designed not to depend on local mobile towers. Those are three defensible claims. “Our app is an operational emergency receiver” is not. ([gsc-europa.eu](https://www.gsc-europa.eu/sites/default/files/sites/all/files/EWSS-CAMF_v1.1.pdf?utm_source=openai))

| **MAJOR — EFAS is not a street-level forecast polygon** | Live medium-range EFAS is restricted to authorised partners; forecasts older than **30 days** are available via EWDS with an ECMWF account and licence acceptance. Use dataset **`efas-forecast`**, not `efas-historical` or a reforecast, for an as-issued replay; the programmatic path is `cdsapi`, typically GRIB2, with experimental NetCDF4 availability. Do not wait for archive jobs or partner credentials, and do not quote lead time. |
| **MAJOR — GFM measures observed surface flooding, not “now”** | CEMS GFM uses Sentinel-1 **IW GRD** SAR scenes; the observed-flood product uses **20 m sampling** and its stated near-real-time target is roughly **within eight hours of acquisition**, not onset. Radar shadow, vegetation, urban effects and excluded/no-observation pixels matter. Sentinel-1C/1D restored nominal **six-day same-track repeat on 24 June 2026**; Sentinel-1A ended operations **29 June 2026**. That repeat is not a revisit or delivery SLA for a specific valley, and it does not describe the 2024 constellation. ([confluence.ecmwf.int](https://confluence.ecmwf.int/spaces/CEMS/pages/288347684/EFAS%2BGlobal%2BFlood%2BMonitoring%2BProducts%2BOverview?utm_source=openai)) | Backup only: use the GFM portal or search collection `GFM` at `https://stac.eodc.eu/api/v1`, asset `ensemble_flood_extent`; the authenticated service API is `https://api.gfm.eodc.eu/v2/`. Portal account and asynchronous export paths exist; don’t assume a quota or completion time. Cache **one** GeoTIFF plus its advisory masks. If there is no usable scene, keep EMSR756; never render excluded pixels as dry. ([extwiki.eodc.eu](https://extwiki.eodc.eu/en/GFM/DataAccess/REST-APIs?utm_source=openai)) |
| **MAJOR — GHSL counts are exposure estimates** | **GHS-POP R2023A**, 100 m World Mollweide (`54009`), is an open, attributed JRC resident-population product, available as tiles without an API key. It estimates residents, not occupants, evacuees, visitors, unique phones, or who lost service. In a narrow valley the results can change materially with cell alignment and hazard-boundary treatment. ([human-settlement.emergency.copernicus.eu](https://human-settlement.emergency.copernicus.eu/downloadWizard.php?utm_source=openai)) | Download the **2020 epoch** tile(s) for a reproducible historical proxy, crop locally, keep fractional counts and mask NoData. Use original population cells, not bilinear interpolation of people-per-cell values. Show a centre-cell estimate and a sensitivity check, such as a **labelled** 100 m hazard buffer or partial-cell allocation. Report rounded **estimated residents** and give the denominator. If the denominator is tiny or zero, switch AOI; don’t rescue it with a hidden buffer. |
| **MAJOR — optimiser can win on paper and lose after encoding** | The v1.1 base latitude/longitude step is about **0.0027466°**, roughly **306 m north–south and 200 m east–west** near the case area. Axis codes are logarithmic and the A16 angle step is **2.8125°**; the spec’s orientation convention, not a GIS bearing default, is authoritative. A centroid-only search can miss a bent corridor; two independent messages may repeat or conflict on a handset. Neither unconstrained best fit nor greedy cover proves a global optimum or gateway support. ([gsc-europa.eu](https://www.gsc-europa.eu/sites/default/files/sites/all/files/EWSS-CAMF_v1.1.pdf)) | Generate candidates from the whole footprint and a few corridor segments. Seed with a metric PCA/oriented fit, enumerate nearby **integer** centre, axis and rotation codes, and **score the decoded** candidates. Use `pyproj` for coordinate transforms/geodesic checks, Shapely for polygon repair/clipping, NumPy for point masks. Verify a north–south and an east–west example before fitting real data. Compare against a **quantised enclosing baseline at the same message count**, then show an optional two-message **union** as a sensitivity case, not a deployment claim. If the proposed ellipse extends beyond available population tiles or EO observation, mark the missing coverage rather than counting it as zero. |
| **MAJOR — the objective can hide missed people** | The “96% / 18% / 11,400” figures are not results. An optimiser can obtain a better over-warning ratio simply by sacrificing a small settlement; people outside the mapped footprint are not necessarily safe. The CEMS data is provided for information, not as a warranted operational hazard determination. ([mapping.emergency.copernicus.eu](https://mapping.emergency.copernicus.eu/terms-and-conditions/?utm_source=openai)) | Make **missed estimated residents** the first number. Show covered/mapped-exposed, missed, additional residents outside the mapped footprint, and that last count divided by estimated residents inside the target. Compare at matched coverage or show the trade-off explicitly. Union/deduplicate candidate masks and show the missed area, even if it worsens the result. Use “best candidate found” and report measured local runtime, not “optimal in under 10 seconds.” |
| **MAJOR — wildfire expansion is not a quick clone** | Sentinel-3 SLSTR `SL_2_FRP___` is catalogued in CDSE at `https://catalogue.dataspace.copernicus.eu/odata/v1/Products`; product download requires an authenticated CDSE path and is quota-limited. The thermal product reports active-fire detections and radiative power, at nominal kilometre-scale thermal sampling, in NetCDF product files—not a fire perimeter, spread model or evacuation zone. EFFIS’s active-fire layer uses **MODIS/VIIRS** detections; EFFIS is a Copernicus service, but those detections are not Sentinel-3 measurements. Neither guarantees a timely overpass. ([documentation.dataspace.copernicus.eu](https://documentation.dataspace.copernicus.eu/APIs/OData.html?utm_source=openai)) | **Cut the second hazard.** If the jury asks about generalisation, show that an authority can swap the input polygon; do not build or claim a fire warning pipeline. Use a second flood AOI for a robustness check only after the main case works. |
| **MAJOR — outage data cannot support the claimed overlay** | PAP’s direct interview with Orange’s spokesperson is dated **16 September 2024** and supports the “nearly half” report; it is **not** an all-operator geospatial coverage audit. PAP reporting from the evening of **15 September** supports loss of mobile coverage in Lądek-Zdrój and partial power, not no power everywhere. Sejm **interpellation 5430** is a parliamentary question, not an independently adjudicated finding. RCB’s own materials, including its October 2026 update, identify **Alert RCB as SMS**, not cell broadcast. Other channels can still exist. ([pap.pl](https://www.pap.pl/aktualnosci/operatorzy-telefonii-komorkowej-czesc-stacji-bazowych-w-kotlinie-klodzkiej-nie-dziala-0?utm_source=openai)) | Keep the dated outage story, **delete the outage layer and weighting**.
