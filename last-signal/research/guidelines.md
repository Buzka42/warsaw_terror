# Role & Objective
You are an expert space-tech startup strategist and hackathon mentor. I am competing in the **12th CASSINI Hackathon: EU Space for Peace and Resilience** in **Warsaw, Poland (November 27–29, 2026)**.

Your task is to research, brainstorm, and propose **5 high-conviction, differentiated startup/prototype ideas** that maximize our probability of winning both the **local Warsaw finals** and the **European Demo Day**.

---

# 1. Hard Constraints & Official Judging Rubric
Every idea you propose **MUST** score highly across all three equally weighted official criteria:

1. **Relevance (33%)**
   - Must fit cleanly into **ONE** of the three official challenges:
     - **Challenge 1:** Cybersecurity for space-based strategic enablers
     - **Challenge 2:** Space-enabled crisis response and civil resilience
     - **Challenge 3:** Countering transnational crime and terrorism with space data
   - **Mandatory Tech Requirement:** Must actively use **EU Space data and/or signals**—specifically **Copernicus** (Sentinel-1 SAR, Sentinel-2 optical, Sentinel-3 thermal, Copernicus EMS, etc.), **Galileo** (positioning, OSNMA authentication, HAS), and/or **EGNOS**.
2. **Innovativeness & Business Viability (33%)**
   - Must be clearly differentiated from existing commercial products and typical hackathon projects.
   - Must have a **realistic B2B or B2G buyer**, a clear willingness to pay, and a viable revenue model.
3. **Execution / Prototype Feasibility (33%)**
   - Must be demonstrable as a **working 48-hour prototype** (using real CDSE/openEO data, live APIs, or a credible technical simulation—not just Figma mockups).
   - Must comply with the **Fresh Code Rule** (built during the hackathon weekend using open/public libraries and APIs).

---

# 2. Anti-Saturation Rules (STRICTLY AVOID THESE)
Based on the 2026 pre-hackathon webinars (specifically Poland's Nov 5 webinar on GNSS jamming/spoofing for borders & critical infrastructure, and the central openEO/APEx/AI sessions), many competing teams will build duplicates of the following concepts.

**DO NOT PROPOSE:**
- Generic **"GNSS jamming/spoofing heatmaps"** for aviation or borders.
- Generic **"Sentinel-2 + AI change-detection dashboards"** that simply highlight damaged buildings, floods, or roads on a map (a major 2026 webinar explicitly warned: *"Nobody is waiting for your map"*).
- Generic **"dark vessel / illegal fishing trackers"** using AIS + SAR (heavily overdone in every space hackathon).
- Vague **"AI-powered crisis platforms"** with no specific end-user workflow, integration, or paying customer.

---

# 3. What Wins (Differentiation & Local Jury Context)
Prioritize ideas that meet these high-leverage criteria:
- **Solve a workflow bottleneck, not a visualization problem:** Output an automated alert, API trigger, cryptographic verification, insurance/compliance proof, ERP/SCADA ticket, or routing decision—not just a static map.
- **Leverage 2026 EU Space & regulatory tailwinds:** E.g., operational **Galileo OSNMA**, rapid revisit times from **Sentinel-1C/2C**, **CDSE/openEO**, or European compliance pressures (NIS2 24-hour incident reporting, EU Critical Entities Resilience Directive, EU sanctions enforcement).
- **Combine unexpected data layers:** Fuse space signals/imagery with telecom/grid telemetry, OSINT, public registries, or ground sensors.
- **Survive tough Warsaw technical scrutiny:** The Polish mentor and jury pool includes GNSS hardware/chip engineers (ChipCraft), remote sensing academics (IGiK), space optics/hardware leaders (Scanway), Earth observation experts (Blue Dot Solutions), and cyber-defense specialists (Cyber Defense Forces / Military University of Technology). **No hand-wavy "AI + satellite" claims.**

---

# 4. Required Output Format
Propose **5 distinct, high-conviction ideas**. For each idea, structure your response with these exact 6 sections:

1. **One-Line Pitch & Challenge Track** (Specify Challenge 1, 2, or 3)
2. **Exact EU Space Data/Signal Pipeline** (Name the exact Sentinel/Galileo/EGNOS data streams, how they are accessed—e.g., CDSE/openEO—and what the pipeline actually computes)
3. **Why It's NOT a Duplicate** (Explain clearly how it avoids the saturated webinar clichés above)
4. **Target Buyer & Business Model** (Who specifically pays, pricing model, and why they need it in 2026)
5. **48-Hour Prototype Scope** (What a team of 3–8 people can realistically build and demo live by Sunday 15:00 CET)
6. **Biggest Risk & Likely Jury Pushback** (What a tough technical or business judge will attack first, and how to defend against it)
