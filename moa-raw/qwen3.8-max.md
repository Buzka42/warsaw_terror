## Idea 1 — "CERTUS": OSNMA-Verified PNT Compliance Layer for NIS2-Critical Entities

**1. One-Line Pitch & Challenge Track**
CERTUS is a middleware agent that cryptographically verifies the Galileo OSNMA authenticity and integrity of every position/time input a critical entity's SCADA, telecom sync, or port-logistics stack depends on — and when verification fails, it auto-drafts the operator's legally mandated NIS2 24-hour early warning and pushes a signed incident record into their SIEM. **Challenge 1: Cybersecurity for space-based strategic enablers.**

**2. Exact EU Space Data/Signal Pipeline**
- **Galileo OSNMA** (free, open, continuously on-air since Initial Service; supported in shipping chipsets from u-blox, Septentrio, STMicro) consumed via an OSNMA-capable receiver (e.g., u-blox ZED-F10T / UM982 evaluation board) through its NMEA/UBX + OSNMA verification API.
- **Galileo timing** (UTC-traceable) from the same receiver as the entity's time-of-record source; optional **Galileo HAS** corrections for cm-level authenticated position where needed.
- Computation: the agent runs a three-way corroboration loop — (a) OSNMA authentication status + ADZD/tesla-key chain state, (b) receiver RAIM/integrity flags, (c) divergence vs. an independent terrestrial reference (PTP grandmaster or NTP-stratum clock). It emits per-second "PNT trust verdicts" (authenticated / degraded / spoofed), hashes them into a tamper-evident Merkle log, and converts verdict transitions into structured SIEM events (CEF/LEEF) plus a pre-filled NIS2 early-warning template (what, when, impact, mitigation — the fields Art. 23 demands).
- No Copernicus needed — but Galileo *is* the EU strategic enabler being defended, which is the literal core of Challenge 1.

**3. Why It's NOT a Duplicate**
The Nov 5 webinar crowd will build **passive jamming heatmaps** — visualization of where C/N₀ dropped. CERTUS produces no map at all: its output is a *cryptographic verdict and a compliance artifact*. It exploits the one thing a jamming detector can't do — **positive authentication**. A jammer tells you signal was lost; OSNMA tells you whether the surviving signal is *genuine*, catching spoofing that a heatmap scores as "healthy signal." And it lands in a workflow (SIEM ticket, regulator report) instead of a dashboard tile.

**4. Target Buyer & Business Model**
Buyers: NIS2 "essential entities" and CER critical entities in Poland — energy DSOs (PGE, Enea, PSE substations using GPS time-sync), telecom operators (5G RAN GNSS timing), ports of Gdańsk/Gdynia (STF/PCS logistics timestamps), and the ~100k entities newly swept into scope by Poland's KSC/NIS2 transposition (2025). They face personal executive liability and turnover-based fines for late or inaccurate incident reports, and their auditors currently have **zero way to evidence PNT integrity**. Pricing: €150–400/site/month SaaS agent + annual "audit trail" export license for regulators/insurers; channel via SIEM integrators and cyber insurers (premium discount for authenticated-PNT sites). Willingness to pay is manufactured by law, not by preference — the strongest possible 2026 tailwind.

**5. 48-Hour Prototype Scope**
Team of 4–5: (a) Hardware person wires up an OSNMA-capable receiver and runs the verification stack (open-source OSNMA libraries exist; u-blox publishes reference code); (b) a GNSS signal generator or software-defined replay attack (legal, shielded/attenuated bench setup — ChipCraft mentors will respect exactly this rigor) to inject spoofed positions; (c) agent service in Python/Go emitting verdicts into **Wazuh** (open-source SIEM) via webhook; (d) NIS2-report generator producing a real-format early-warning PDF/JSON within seconds of a spoof event; (e) live demo: attacker starts replaying → dashboard-free terminal shows verdict flip → Wazuh alert fires → 24h-report draft is generated with signed evidence chain → show the Merkle log verification. Sunday 15:00 demo is a **live attack, defeated and documented end-to-end**.

**6. Biggest Risk & Likely Jury Pushback**
Attack 1 (technical, from ChipCraft/Cyber Defense Forces judges): "Receiver vendors already implement OSNMA — what do you add?" Defense: receivers verify *signals*, not *organizational state*; nobody bridges verification output to SIEM correlation, NIS2 reporting deadlines, multi-receiver fleet consensus, or audit evidence. That integration layer is where enterprise money lives (cf. how EDR sits atop OS primitives). Attack 2 (business): "NIS2 entities buy from big SOC vendors, not startups." Defense: we ship as an OEM module/API to those vendors and to GNSS receiver makers first — B2B2B, with the demo proving the API is a two-week integration. Attack 3: legality of the spoofing demo — resolve *before the weekend* with organizers using bench-level attenuated transmission or recorded/replayed IQ samples.

## Idea 2 — "TorGuard": Declared-vs-Observed Rail Consignment Risk Triage at the EU's Eastern Rail Gate

**1. One-Line Pitch & Challenge Track**
TorGuard fuses Sentinel-1/2-observed activity in rail yards like Małaszewicze (the EU's largest China–Europe dry port, on the Belarus border) with schedules, manifests, and OSINT to risk-score consignments for sanctions evasion and smuggling, and pushes a prioritized **inspection queue** into customs officers' workflow — instead of another map nobody opens. **Challenge 3: Countering transnational crime and terrorism with space data.**

**2. Exact EU Space Data/Signal Pipeline**
- **Sentinel-1 IW GRD + SLC** over Małaszewicze/Terespol/Braniewo yards via **CDSE STAC API + openEO backend** (S1C generation gives dense European revisit); computation: per-yard time series of backscatter change detection and train-presence/consist-length estimation on marshalling tracks using a lightweight CV model (open-weights segmentation, fine-tuned at the hackathon on labeled S2 chips).
- **Sentinel-2 L2A** via CDSE openEO for optical confirmation (wagon counting, container stack changes) on cloud-free passes.
- Fusion layer: public rail schedules/transit declarations (UTLC ERA timetables, OSINT transshipment reports) + mocked customs manifest stream → a **declared-vs-observed divergence score** per yard per day ("manifest says 3 trains cleared Tuesday; SAR shows 5 consisted in the yard"). Divergence + anomaly type → risk-ranked triage list exposed as REST API.
- **Galileo OSNMA** used in the field-inspection component: officers' tablet captures and position stamps get authenticated, so the evidence package can't be challenged as tampered.

**3. Why It's NOT a Duplicate**
It's not vessel dark-tracking (wrong domain, wrong buyer), not a change-detection dashboard (the map is internal; the product is a **ranked work queue with a divergence score**), and not a generic "AI platform" — the end user is a specific KAS/OLAF officer deciding *which of 200 consignments to open today*. The core innovation is the **reconciliation primitive**: space data as ground truth against *declared paperwork*. Every existing customs tool trusts the paperwork; every existing EO tool ignores it.

**4. Target Buyer & Business Model**
Primary: **KAS (National Revenue Administration)/CBŚP** and EU-level **OLAF/DG TAXUD** — sanctions circumvention via rail transshipment is a top-2026 enforcement priority (dual-use goods, sanctioned Belarusian/Russian potash, timber "laundering" through third-country paperwork), and the incoming EU Customs Authority will need exactly this analytic layer. Secondary commercial buyer: terminal operators (e.g., Cargotor) who get a **green-lane throughput product** — low-risk trains waved through faster. Pricing: B2G per-terminal license €80–150k/yr + per-inspection evidence-package fees for prosecutors; B2B ops tier for terminals. Why pay in 2026: inspection capacity is the binding constraint — officers can open maybe 2% of containers, and every wrong pick is a political/press event.

**5. 48-Hour Prototype Scope**
Team of 5–6: (a) pull 6 months of S1 GRD + S2 over one yard from CDSE openEO (scripted day 1); (b) train/tune a wagon-and-train detector on a few hundred manually labeled chips (labeling party Friday night); (c) build the yard activity time series + coherent-change anomaly flags; (d) scrape UTLC/public schedules into a mock manifest stream; (e) divergence-scoring service + a **triage console** (queue view, per-consignment evidence timeline, one-click OSNMA-stamped evidence pack export); (f) demo script: replay a real historical week where OSINT reported suspicious transshipment, show the system flagging it *before* the news, show the officer's queue reordering live.

**6. Biggest Risk & Likely Jury Pushback**
Attack 1 (IGiK/Scanway technical judges): "You can't count individual wagons or read container numbers from Sentinel-1 at 5 m — this is science fiction." Defense: we never claim wagon-level ID; the validated signal is **yard-level consist presence, length, and dwell-time anomaly** (well within S1 capability, demonstrated on chips during the pitch), with S2 as opportunistically optical confirmation, and a human-in-the-loop officer making the call. We sell *prioritization* (base rate improvement), not adjudication. Attack 2: "Customs manifests are classified — you'll never get the data." Defense: divergence works against *publicly declared* schedules and transit T1 filings; and EU customs data spaces (2026 customs reform) are explicitly designed to share risk analytics. Prototype ships with a realistic mock + a signed-LOI-style integration story. Attack 3: dual-use/misuse optics (surveillance state) — preempt with a privacy-by-design slide: no personal data, infrastructure-scale only, audit-logged.

## Idea 3 — "Veritrace": Court-Admissible Evidence Chain-of-Custody for Illegal Waste Trafficking

**1. One-Line Pitch & Challenge Track**
Veritrace reconciles Poland's waste-transport registries (SENT/BDO) against what Copernicus actually sees on the ground at disposal sites, and auto-assembles a **cryptographically sealed, court-admissible evidence package** — turning a 6-month prosecutor file into a same-day artifact. **Challenge 3: Countering transnational crime and terrorism with space data.**

**2. Exact EU Space Data/Signal Pipeline**
- **Sentinel-2 L2A** via CDSE (openEO batch jobs): biweekly change detection at *declared* disposal/recycling sites (parcel polygons from BDO registry) — new stockpile growth, burning scars (NBR/SWIR indices), activity at sites declared dormant.
- **Sentinel-1 GRD** via CDSE for all-weather backscatter change (soil disturbance, new piles) — Poland's 60%+ cloud cover makes SAR the workhorse.
- **Galileo OSNMA** for the field leg: inspector photos/drone geotags and tracker positions are captured with authenticated positioning, so defense counsel can't argue the coordinates were falsified.
- Evidence assembly service: pulls the exact **STAC items** (with processing graphs) used in each finding, stamps derivatives with **C2PA Content Credentials**, adds an **eIDAS qualified timestamp**, anchors digests to **EBSI/European Blockchain**, and emits a verification portal URL/QR any judge, insurer, or journalist can independently check.
- Fusion with SENT transport telemetry (already integrated with BDO in Poland): route-declaration vs. authenticated-actual-path mismatch flags "midnight dumping" candidates.

**3. Why It's NOT a Duplicate**
The cliché is "Sentinel-2 + AI highlights something illegal on a map." Veritrace assumes detection is the *easy* 20% and attacks the real bottleneck every WIOŚ inspector and prosecutor will confirm: **evidence dies in court** — chain-of-custody gaps, unverifiable geolocation, unauthenticated processing provenance. No hackathon team will build the C2PA + eIDAS + OSNMA + STAC-provenance spine; that's a regulatory-engineering product, and it's the only part that creates willingness to pay. The satellite layer is one witness among several (registry, telemetry, field capture) — precisely the "unexpected data-layer fusion" the brief rewards.

**4. Target Buyer & Business Model**
Primary: **WIOŚ/GIOŚ** (environmental inspectorates), **KAS**, and prosecutors' offices — illegal waste trafficking is an EMPACT priority, Polish courts see multi-million-PLN dumping cases yearly (imported waste, abandoned "bomb" sites), and the new **EU Waste Shipment Regulation (applies 2026)** massively raises the compliance/evidence burden. Secondary: municipal waste companies (compliance proof), environmental-liability insurers, remediation firms bidding public tenders. Pricing: B2G per-evidence-package (€2–8k) + annual reconciliation-engine subscription per voivodeship (€40–80k); B2B compliance tier for waste operators who must *prove* legal disposal. Why 2026: WSR obligations + post-scandal political pressure + courts already skeptical of EO evidence create simultaneous push and pull.

**5. 48-Hour Prototype Scope**
Team of 4–6: (a) pick 2–3 real documented dump sites (press-reported coordinates) + 1 legal site as control; (b) openEO batch job pulling S1+S2 time series, run open-source change detection (documented, reproducible graph); (c) mock-but-realistic SENT/BDO feed (public schema) with one injected fraudulent route; (d) OSNMA-stamped field capture via receiver + phone during the weekend (an actual team member plays the inspector); (e) evidence-assembly service producing a sealed PDF/ZIP bundle + public verification page with C2PA check and eIDAS timestamp validation; (f) demo: live "raid" Friday → Sunday presentation walks the bundle through a mock courtroom challenge ("how do you know this pixel wasn't Photoshopped?") and answers it with three cryptographic verifications on stage.

**6. Biggest Risk & Likely Jury Pushback**
Attack 1 (legal/prosecutor mindset): "Polish courts have no procedure for admitting C2PA/EBSI artifacts." Defense: qualified timestamps (eIDAS) are *already* legally recognized; C2PA is an integrity layer on top of a normal expert-opinion workflow — we don't replace the biegły (expert witness), we make their provenance unimpeachable; pilot with one cooperative prosecutor's office as design partner. Attack 2: "Detection accuracy on 10 m pixels for small dumps is low." Defense: we never auto-accuse — the engine produces *leads* for inspection; the value is in reconciliation at scale (thousands of registered sites, biweekly) and the evidence spine once a human confirms. Attack 3: registry access (SENT/BDO APIs are permissioned) — defense: the SENT–BDO integration pipe already exists institutionally; prototype uses faithful mocks built from public schemas, and our buyer *is* the registry operator (GIOŚ).

## Idea 4 — "Baltic Corridor": SLA-Grade Watch & Incident-Reporting Autopilot for Subsea Cables and Pipelines

**1. One-Line Pitch & Challenge Track**
Baltic Corridor is an always-on service that scores every vessel interaction with a specific subsea cable/pipeline corridor in the southern Baltic and, on threshold breach, simultaneously alerts the asset owner's duty desk, notifies MRCC/Navy, and auto-generates the owner's CER incident-report draft — the compliance-and-alerting autopilot asset owners are legally obliged to want. **Challenge 2: Space-enabled crisis response and civil resilience.**

**2. Exact EU Space Data/Signal Pipeline**
- **Sentinel-1 IW GRD** via **CDSE STAC/openEO**: scheduled ship-detection passes over pre-registered corridor polygons (public cable-route charts + maritime office concession data — e.g., HVDC interconnector routes, Arelion/Telia fiber landings, Baltic Pipe corridor, planned PSE Baltic links) at every available acquisition (S1C/S1D generation = multi-day, often sub-daily over Europe in emergency tasking).
- Live **AIS stream** (AISStream.io / AISHub websocket) fused with SAR detections: the key computation is the **SAR-vs-AIS mismatch matrix** (non-transmitting vessel inside a protection zone), plus kinematic classifiers — anchor-drag over burial depth, loitering >N hours, dredger/trawler gear signatures, course-over-cable geometry.
- **Galileo OSNMA** for authenticated positioning of patrol/ROV response units, so response logs are spoof-proof evidence.
- Outputs per event: severity score, corridor-impact probability, machine-readable alert (webhook/SMS/email to duty officer), and an auto-assembled **CER/NIS2-style incident record + evidence timeline** (SAR scene IDs, AIS track replay hash, timestamps) for the regulator and the insurer.

**3. Why It's NOT a Duplicate**
Dark-vessel trackers (Windward, Spire, every hackathon AIS+SAR project) sell *maritime domain awareness to navies and fisheries agencies* as a map/product. Baltic Corridor sells **an SLA to a named asset owner**: your cable, your corridor polygon, your duty desk, your regulator report, your insurance claim file. The unit of product is not a vessel but a *corridor-asset interaction with contractual response deadlines*. Nobody at the hackathon will pre-register actual Baltic cable corridors and wire graded alerts into a duty-desk + incident-reporting workflow — that's the unsexy 80% that makes it a business rather than a demo.

**4. Target Buyer & Business Model**
Buyers: (a) subsea asset owners — TSOs (PSE, Litgrid), telecom carriers (Arelion, Telia), Gaz-System (Baltic Pipe), offshore wind export-cable operators (Orlen, Baltic Power — dozens of GW under construction); (b) their **insurers/underwriters** (subsea cable claims post-Estlink2/C-Lion1 are driving premiums up — insurers will subsidize monitoring); (c) maritime administrations/MRCC as public tier. Why 2026: NATO Baltic Sentry, the EU undersea-infrastructure resilience toolbox, and CER transposition put named legal duties on these owners for the first time — they must *detect, report, and evidence*. Pricing: per-corridor-km SaaS (€5–15k per corridor per year), insurer co-pay model, incident-package fees for claims. Beachhead: Poland's own Baltic assets → pitch lands hard with a Warsaw jury.

**5. 48-Hour Prototype Scope**
Team of 4–6: (a) digitize 3 real corridors from public cable maps Friday morning (GeoJSON, ~2h); (b) CDSE openEO scheduled job pulling S1 GRD, ship detection with an open model, results to PostGIS; (c) AIS websocket ingesting live Baltic traffic into the same store; (d) rules/scoring engine (mismatch, loitering, anchor-drag, dredger heuristic) → graded alerts; (e) duty-desk console (minimal: event feed, corridor status, one-click report generator) + Telegram/webhook notifications; (f) **killer demo**: replay the real Estlink-2 incident window (Dec 2024, Eagle S) through the pipeline and show the alert + incident report it *would have* produced in minutes — then flip to live Baltic traffic running in real time on stage.

**6. Biggest Risk & Likely Jury Pushback**
Attack 1: "SAR revisit latency (hours) makes this useless against a sabotage happening now — AIS/radar already do real-time." Defense: agreed and stated up front — real-time layer is AIS; SAR's job is the **non-cooperative gap** (AIS off/spoofed, which is exactly the Eagle S pattern) and the **all-weather evidence record** for claims/prosecution; S1C/D tasking narrows latency; fusion is the product. Attack 2: "Owners won't share cable coordinates." Defense: corridors are already published in maritime concession charts and cable maps; sensitive burial-depth data stays client-side — we ingest their KMZ, we never publish it. Attack 3 (business judge): "Windward/Spire have the data moat." Defense: they're horizontal platforms; asset owners with a named legal duty buy *vertical workflow with SLA and regulator-ready output* — and incumbents' pricing excludes mid-tier TSOs. Have the duty-desk workflow demo ready as the answer.

## Idea 5 — "GruntWatch": Millimeter-Scale InSAR Autopilot for Flood Embankments and Dams → Automated Work Orders

**1. One-Line Pitch & Challenge Track**
GruntWatch turns every Sentinel-1 pass over Poland's flood embankments and dams into an automated deformation verdict — and when an embankment segment moves or loses coherence, it files a **maintenance work order into the operator's ERP** and appends a regulatory inspection record, so water infrastructure is inspected by exception instead of by boots in the rain. **Challenge 2: Space-enabled crisis response and civil resilience.**

**2. Exact EU Space Data/Signal Pipeline**
- **Sentinel-1 SLC (IW)** downloaded via **CDSE STAC/OData API**; processed with **open-source ESA SNAP (gpt scripting)** — fresh-code-compliant tooling — computing differential interferograms on short-baseline pairs plus **coherence maps** per embankment segment polygon (Wody Polskie asset registers, OSM flood-defense ways).
- Two computed signals per segment per pass: (a) line-of-sight displacement (mm-scale) on persistent scatterers near the crest/berm; (b) **coherence loss** = surface disturbance (saturation, sloughing, rodent/burrow activity, illegal excavation) — often visible *before* displacement.
- **Sentinel-2 L2A** (CDSE openEO): NDWI/soil-moisture proxies on the slope face to weight alerts after heavy rain; **Sentinel-3 SLSTR** land-surface temperature as a seepage indicator in frozen/thaw conditions (secondary, honest about resolution).
- Threshold engine → JSON work orders (segment ID, movement vector, confidence, suggested inspection class) into a mock ERP/maintenance system + an append-only inspection log satisfying CER/Prawo wodne documentation duties.
- Optional **Galileo OSNMA** stamping on the inspector's field verification photos, closing the loop.

**3. Why It's NOT a Duplicate**
It is emphatically *not* post-disaster flood mapping (the saturated cliché) — it is **pre-failure prevention**: the September 2024 flood killed the Stronie Śląskie dry dam and overtopped dozens of Wałbrzych-region embankments; every one had measurable precursors. The output is not a colored map but a **ticket in an ERP and a row in a compliance log** — the brief's exact "solve the workflow bottleneck" criterion. And InSAR geodesy is a discipline the Warsaw jury (IGiK is a European InSAR center) will recognize as real engineering, not "AI + satellite" hand-waving — which cuts both ways (see §6) and rewards rigor.

**4. Target Buyer & Business Model**
Primary: **PGW Wody Polskie** (operates ~thousands of km of embankments and 100+ dams with chronically underfunded inspection crews — their statutory przeglądy are periodic and manual), hydro-plant dam operators (Tauron, Enea, PGE), and voivodeship crisis-management centers. Secondary: **reinsurers and Polish property insurers** pricing flood cover on diked areas (a coherence/displacement history is actuarial gold), and engineering firms doing dam safety reviews. Why 2026: post-2024 flood reconstruction program (billions PLN for hydrotechnical works) + CER transposition putting named reporting duties on water-critical entities + a labor shortage of field inspectors. Pricing: per-monitored-km SaaS (€200–500/km/yr — orders of magnitude below survey campaigns or commercial corner-reflector services), work-order integration fee, insurer data-license tier.

**5. 48-Hour Prototype Scope**
Team of 4–6: (a) pre-select 3 real segments Friday: a Vistula embankment near Warsaw, Racibórz reservoir, one 2024-flood-damaged site with known outcome; (b) scripted CDSE SLC fetch + SNAP gpt interferometry chain (coregistration → interferogram → coherence → DInSAR phase) — run pairs in parallel on team laptops; (c) segment polygon join + threshold engine + alert records; (d) ERP mock (or real open CMMS) receiving work-order JSON; (e) **validation demo**: run the pipeline on 2024 historical acquisitions over Stronie Śląskie/Racibórz and show the system flagging precursors *retrospectively against known ground truth* — then show the live pipeline issuing this week's ticket. Honest demo framing: "demo-grade pair processing now; MintPy time-series stack is the productization path."

**6. Biggest Risk & Likely Jury Pushback**
Attack 1 (IGiK judges, guaranteed): "Atmospheric delay, temporal decorrelation on vegetated embankments, and orbital error will swamp mm signals on short stacks — you're presenting noise." Defense (must be airtight): scope claims to **coherence-loss detection** (robust) as primary signal and displacement as secondary on PS-rich segments (crest roads, concrete structures); show coherence validated against a known 2024 failure; cite that operational PS-InSAR products for dikes exist in NL/DE (Hansje Brinker, DGM) — proving feasibility *and* proving nobody offers it as an automated Polish work-order product. Attack 2: "48h InSAR won't finish computing." Defense: pre-planned pairs, GRD fallback for the coherence product, parallel laptops; the pipeline is scripted Saturday morning, Sunday is rehearsal. Attack 3 (business): "Wody Polskie procurement is glacial." Defense: wedge via insurers and engineering consultancies (private payers of the same data) + post-flood reconstruction program funding window; the ERP integration demo exists precisely to show buyer-workflow fit, not just science.
